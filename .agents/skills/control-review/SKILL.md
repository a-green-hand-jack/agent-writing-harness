# Control Review

## Trigger

Use before changing a central claim, story choice, experiment condition, limitation, result interpretation, or stable paper interface.

## Minimum context

- the relevant section of `PAPER.md`;
- `EXPERIMENTS.md` only for experiment or result changes;
- `PAPER_INTERFACES.md` only for recurring semantic interfaces;
- the active text and relevant current decision.

Do not load unrelated venue, release, history, or ledger material.

## Procedure

1. Identify what meaning could change.
2. Classify the current cue as locked, bounded, free, or unresolved.
3. For free, make the low-risk edit and report it.
4. For bounded, confirm the written boundary and stop if the change exceeds it.
5. For locked, prepare a proposal rather than modifying the meaning.
6. For unresolved, prefer reversible progress; ask before a high-impact or hard-to-reverse choice.
7. List affected sections, experiments, interfaces, and limitations.

## Human decision

Request a Human decision when scientific meaning, claim strength, fairness, claim degradation, required negative evidence, or interface meaning may change.
