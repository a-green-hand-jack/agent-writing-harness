# Project Writing DNA

This directory governs how the bundled `writing-dna-skill` is adapted to
academic paper writing in this template. It is downstream-local project
knowledge: it is generated by the Human and Agent from a reviewed corpus, and
it is protected from template-sync overwrite.

## Workflow

1. **Corpus.** The Human selects reference papers (or the Agent proposes with
   Human approval). Source PDFs stay outside Git or under ignored
   `.agents/runtime/writing-dna/`; never commit paper full texts.
2. **Distill.** Load `.agents/skills/writing-dna-skill/SKILL.md` and follow the
   academic adaptation below. Optionally use
   `.agents/skills/ccf-paper-to-exemplar/SKILL.md` to produce section-level
   exemplar cards from each paper first.
3. **Candidate.** Distilled rules are written under ignored
   `.agents/runtime/writing-dna/` as candidates, not durable project truth.
4. **Human review.** The Human approves or rejects the candidate with
   `style-alignment` review. Only the approved result is promoted to
   `paper-writing-dna.md` in this directory.
5. **Activation.** The Writing DNA takes effect only after the Human activates
   it in `PAPER.md` (Writing style section). Until then it is reference
   material only.

## Academic adaptation

`writing-dna-skill` was designed for blogs and media accounts. For papers,
distill only:

- rhetorical and paragraph moves (claim → evidence → qualification ordering);
- section-level responsibility patterns (abstract moves, introduction roles,
  method presentation, evidence organization);
- sentence-level density, voice, hedging, and transition preferences;
- citation weaving and evidence-presentation patterns;
- caption and figure/table narrative conventions;
- anti-patterns to avoid.

Do not inherit from the corpus: viewpoints, claims, terminology, citation
choices, technical content, or a named author's personal identity. The paper's
own contracts (`PAPER.md`, `EXPERIMENTS.md`, `PAPER_INTERFACES.md`) always take
precedence over any distilled style.

When an exemplar card's "reusable technique" conflicts with a Writing-DNA rule, the Human-approved Writing DNA wins; cards are source material, DNA is the approved distillation.

## Output contract

A promoted `paper-writing-dna.md` must state: the corpus identity (titles and
sources, not full text), what it may influence, what it must never change
(claim strength, terminology meaning, limitations, interface meaning), and its
activation status. Keep it concise and rule-shaped, not a prose essay.
